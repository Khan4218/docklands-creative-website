# Rouge Media — Website Benchmark

## Competitor
Rouge Media

## Benchmark Purpose

Rouge Media was reviewed as part of the Docklands Creative competitor
benchmarking exercise. The assessment focused on website experience,
creative and digital positioning, service communication, portfolio
presentation and opportunities relevant to the proposed redesign.

## Key Strengths

- Strong creative and digital agency positioning
- Professional website presentation
- Clear communication of digital services
- Strong portfolio and project-led communication
- Good balance between creative presentation and business messaging
- Established and credible agency positioning

## Key Weaknesses / Opportunities

- A professional agency presentation can be strengthened further through
  clearer differentiation and stronger positioning.
- Docklands Creative can create a more distinctive identity by combining
  creative services with a stronger technology and digital-growth narrative.
- The redesign can make the customer journey more direct through clearer
  calls to action and service pathways.

## Strategic Lessons for Docklands Creative

1. Combine creative presentation with clear business communication.
2. Use portfolio projects to demonstrate credibility and capability.
3. Make service categories easy for prospective clients to understand.
4. Use prominent calls to action to support conversion.
5. Create a consistent visual system across all website sections.
6. Differentiate the agency through a clear and memorable positioning
   statement.

## Benchmark Position

Rouge Media was positioned strongly on:

- Professional presentation
- Creative capability
- Digital services
- Portfolio communication
- Market positioning

## Relevance to Final Website

The Rouge Media benchmark informed the decision to create a professional,
conversion-focused website for Docklands Creative that combines strong
creative presentation with clear services, project evidence and calls to
action.

## Source

Competitor website review and benchmark analysis conducted as part of the
Docklands Creative website redesign project.
