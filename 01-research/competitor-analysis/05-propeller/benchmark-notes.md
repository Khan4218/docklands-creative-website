# Propeller — Website Benchmark

## Competitor
Propeller

## Benchmark Purpose

Propeller was reviewed as part of the Docklands Creative competitor
benchmarking exercise. The assessment focused on digital capability,
creative positioning, website experience, service communication and
market positioning.

## Key Strengths

- Strong digital and creative positioning
- Professional and contemporary agency presentation
- Clear emphasis on digital solutions
- Strong creative and technical capability
- Effective communication of agency expertise
- Premium market positioning

## Key Weaknesses / Opportunities

- A premium positioning can create a perceived distance from smaller
  businesses looking for accessible digital support.
- Docklands Creative can differentiate by combining premium presentation
  with clearer explanations of services, process and business outcomes.
- The redesigned website can make the customer journey more direct and
  conversion-focused.

## Strategic Lessons for Docklands Creative

1. Communicate digital expertise immediately through the hero section.
2. Combine creative quality with evidence of technical capability.
3. Present services in a way that is easy for prospective clients to
   understand.
4. Use selected projects to demonstrate both creative and digital
   competence.
5. Maintain a premium visual identity without making the website feel
   inaccessible.
6. Use strong calls to action to move visitors towards enquiries.

## Benchmark Position

Propeller was positioned strongly on:

- Digital capability
- Creative capability
- Technical expertise
- Premium positioning
- Professional presentation

## Relevance to Final Website

The Propeller benchmark informed the decision to position Docklands Creative
as a premium but accessible digital creative agency. The redesigned website
combines strong visual presentation with clear services, project evidence,
business outcomes and conversion-focused calls to action.

## Source

Competitor website review and benchmark analysis conducted as part of the
Docklands Creative website redesign project.
