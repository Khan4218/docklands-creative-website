# Burst Digital — Website Benchmark

## Competitor
Burst Digital

## Benchmark Purpose

Burst Digital was reviewed as part of the Docklands Creative competitor
benchmarking exercise. The assessment focused on digital capability, website
experience, service communication, creative positioning and opportunities
relevant to the proposed redesign.

## Key Strengths

- Strong digital-first positioning
- Clear focus on digital marketing and online growth
- Modern and energetic agency presentation
- Strong communication of digital services
- Good emphasis on results and business growth

## Key Weaknesses / Opportunities

- A strong digital-marketing focus can make the agency positioning feel
  narrower than a broader creative and digital studio.
- Docklands Creative can differentiate through a wider combination of web
  design, branding, e-commerce and digital marketing.
- The redesigned website can communicate the relationship between creative
  work and measurable business outcomes more clearly.

## Strategic Lessons for Docklands Creative

1. Communicate digital capability clearly from the first screen.
2. Connect services to business outcomes rather than listing services alone.
3. Use concise, action-oriented messaging throughout the website.
4. Demonstrate capability through selected projects and evidence.
5. Maintain a modern digital aesthetic while keeping the experience easy to
   navigate.

## Benchmark Position

Burst Digital was positioned strongly on:

- Digital capability
- Growth-oriented communication
- Digital marketing
- Modern agency presentation

## Relevance to Final Website

The Burst Digital benchmark informed the decision to position Docklands
Creative as a modern digital agency capable of connecting website design,
branding, e-commerce and digital marketing with practical business outcomes.

## Source

Competitor website review and benchmark analysis conducted as part of the
Docklands Creative website redesign project.
