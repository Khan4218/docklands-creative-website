# Dusted — Website Benchmark

## Competitor
Dusted

## Benchmark Purpose

Dusted was reviewed as part of the Docklands Creative competitor benchmarking
exercise. The assessment focused on website experience, creative positioning,
digital capability and opportunities relevant to the proposed redesign.

## Key Strengths

- Strong creative agency positioning
- Distinctive visual identity
- Clear presentation of creative capability
- Engaging portfolio-oriented communication
- Strong emphasis on brand and digital experience

## Key Weaknesses / Opportunities

- Highly creative presentation can sometimes prioritise visual impact over
  detailed business information.
- Docklands Creative can differentiate by communicating services, processes
  and measurable outcomes more explicitly.
- The redesign can combine creative personality with stronger usability and
  business-focused messaging.

## Strategic Lessons for Docklands Creative

1. Develop a distinctive and memorable visual identity.
2. Use creative presentation to communicate agency capability.
3. Showcase projects as evidence of expertise rather than simply displaying
   visual work.
4. Balance creativity with clear navigation and accessible business
   information.
5. Maintain a consistent visual language across the website.

## Benchmark Position

Dusted was positioned strongly on:

- Creative identity
- Visual differentiation
- Creative communication
- Digital/brand presentation

## Relevance to Final Website

The Dusted benchmark informed the decision to make Docklands Creative visually
distinctive while retaining clear service communication, project evidence and
business-oriented messaging.

## Source

Competitor website review and benchmark analysis conducted as part of the
Docklands Creative website redesign project.
