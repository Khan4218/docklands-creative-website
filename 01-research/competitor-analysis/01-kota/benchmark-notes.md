# KOTA — Website Benchmark

## Competitor
KOTA

## Benchmark Purpose

KOTA was reviewed as part of the Docklands Creative competitor benchmarking
exercise. The assessment focused on website experience, digital capability,
visual positioning and opportunities relevant to the redesign.

## Key Strengths

- Strong digital-first positioning
- High perceived innovation and technology capability
- Premium visual presentation
- Clear emphasis on digital experience
- Strong portfolio-led communication

## Key Weaknesses / Opportunities

- The premium digital positioning can create distance from smaller business
  audiences.
- The experience can be used as a benchmark for innovation without directly
  copying its visual approach.
- Docklands Creative can differentiate through clearer communication of
  services, process and outcomes.

## Strategic Lessons for Docklands Creative

1. Present the agency as digitally capable and forward-looking.
2. Use strong visual hierarchy to communicate capability quickly.
3. Make portfolio work a central part of the website experience.
4. Combine premium presentation with accessible business-focused messaging.

## Benchmark Position

KOTA was positioned strongly on:

- Innovation
- Digital capability
- Premium positioning

## Relevance to Final Website

The KOTA benchmark informed the decision to give Docklands Creative a stronger
digital and technology-oriented visual identity while maintaining a clear,
business-focused message.

## Source

Competitor website review and benchmark analysis conducted as part of the
Docklands Creative website redesign project.
