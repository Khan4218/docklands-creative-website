# Docklands Creative — Master Competitor Benchmark Workbook

## Purpose

This workbook consolidates the competitor website benchmarking undertaken
for the Docklands Creative website redesign.

The benchmark applies the same criteria across Docklands Creative and five
competitor agencies:

1. Dusted
2. KOTA
3. Burst Digital
4. Rouge Media
5. Propeller

The purpose is to identify industry best practices, competitive gaps and
website features that should inform the proposed Docklands Creative redesign.

---

# 1. Benchmark Method

Each website was evaluated against the same 14 website capability criteria.

### Scoring system

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | Available / Fully Implemented |
| 2 | Partially Available / Limited |
| 1 | Not Available / Missing |

Maximum possible score:

**14 criteria × 3 = 42 points**

The score can subsequently be converted into a percentage:

**Benchmark % = Score ÷ 42 × 100**

This approach supports consistent comparison across all competitors.

# 4. Docklands Creative Competitive Gaps

The benchmark identifies the following major areas for improvement:

## High-Priority Gaps

### Detailed Case Studies
Docklands Creative currently lacks detailed case-study presentation compared
with all five benchmark competitors.

### Client Logos
Competitors provide stronger visible evidence of previous clients and
organisational credibility.

### Consultation Booking
Competitors provide a clearer pathway for prospective clients to initiate
contact or consultation.

### Awards & Recognition
Docklands Creative has limited visible recognition compared with most
competitors.

---

## Medium-Priority Gaps

### Project Portfolio
The existing portfolio requires stronger presentation and evidence of work.

### Client Testimonials
Testimonials are currently limited and should be strengthened as trust
signals.

### Blog / Insights
A stronger content and insights section could support authority and SEO.

### SEO Content
The redesigned website should provide stronger search-oriented content
architecture.

### Multiple CTAs
Calls to action should be distributed more strategically across the customer
journey.

### Social Media Integration
Social proof and external digital presence can be integrated more clearly.

### Accessibility
Accessibility should be considered systematically throughout the redesign.

# 6. Design Implications for Docklands Creative

The benchmark directly informs the proposed website redesign.

### 01 — Stronger Homepage

Create a clear value proposition explaining:

**What Docklands Creative does + who it serves + the value it creates.**

### 02 — Stronger Services Architecture

Clearly communicate:

- Web Design & Development
- Branding & Identity
- E-commerce
- Digital Marketing & SEO

### 03 — Project Evidence

Develop a stronger portfolio system with selected projects and visual
case-study previews.

### 04 — Case Studies

Introduce detailed case studies covering:

- Client challenge
- Strategy
- Design
- Development
- Outcome

### 05 — Trust Signals

Strengthen:

- Client logos
- Testimonials
- Awards/recognition
- Project evidence

### 06 — Conversion

Introduce stronger calls to action such as:

**Start a Project**

**Book a Consultation**

**View Our Work**

### 07 — Content & SEO

Introduce an Insights / News section to demonstrate expertise and support
organic visibility.

### 08 — Accessibility

Ensure the final website considers accessibility, responsive behaviour,
readability and clear navigation.

---

# 7. Benchmark Conclusion

The benchmark indicates that Docklands Creative has a substantial
opportunity to strengthen its digital presence.

The five competitors demonstrate a consistently stronger implementation of
portfolio evidence, case studies, trust signals, content, conversion
features and supporting digital capabilities.

The redesign therefore should not simply improve visual appearance.

It should improve the complete customer journey:

**Discover → Understand → Trust → Explore → Enquire**

The benchmark findings will be carried forward into the competitive gap
analysis and website redesign recommendations.
