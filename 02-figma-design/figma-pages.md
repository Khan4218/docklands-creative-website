# Docklands Creative – Figma Page Structure

## Purpose

This document records the planned internal website pages developed from the approved Docklands Creative landing-page design.

## Landing Page

The landing page establishes the visual identity of the redesigned website.

Key sections include:

- Hero section
- Trusted brands
- Digital solutions/services
- Selected projects
- Value proposition
- Design process
- Client testimonial
- Insights/news
- Call-to-action
- Footer

## Internal Page 1 – Services

Purpose:

- Service process
- Benefits
- CTA

## Design Consistency

All internal pages will maintain the approved visual language of the landing page:

- Dark navy backgrounds
- White content sections
- Orange primary CTA
- Pink/magenta accent
- Rounded cards
- Strong typography
- Generous spacing
- Consistent navigation
- Responsive layouts

## Development Relationship

The Figma pages will act as the visual reference for the subsequent WordPress/custom development stage.

The final implementation will be compared against the approved Figma designs to maintain visual consistency.
